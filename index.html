The problem

Most people know what they should do. They don’t do it.

Not because they’re lazy, and not because the information is hard to find. There’s more free money advice out there than anyone could read. What’s missing is a system to follow, people to do it with, and a reason to start today instead of someday. That’s the gap The Forge is built to close.

Why it sticks

My degree is in psychology. The thing I studied hardest is why capable people stay stuck.

So the courses are built around how people actually change, not how we think we should.

    Change starts with who you believe you are. The habits follow.

    You figure it out by doing it, not by thinking about doing it.

  • Small reps compound

    A few non-negotiables, done daily, quietly stack over time.
